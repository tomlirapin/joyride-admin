Delete your JoyRide account

Updated November 12, 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

In the app

  1. Open JoyRide and tap your avatar in the search bar to open your profile.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings), then Contact support.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email testing@joyrideapp.co from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my JoyRide account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

Your profile, sign-in, avatar pictures, drives you saved to your account, road ratings, saved roads, friends and ride invites. This can’t be undone.

What we keep

  • Bug reports and messages you sent keep their text, but no longer show your name or email.
  • Drive reports from testing are deleted after 90 days.

Drives and settings stored only on your phone go away when you delete the app.
