Privacy Policy

Updated October 19, 2026

Terms of Use

What we collect

If you sign in: your email address, and for Google sign-in the name and profile picture URL Google provides (we do not display the picture). Your chosen avatar, ride and driving style. Road ratings and surface notes you submit, tied to a random device identifier rather than your name. With your permission, your location while the app is open, used on the device to show the map around you.

What stays on your phone

Recorded drives (route, times, speeds and cornering), your own ratings list, and preferences are stored on your phone. They are not uploaded unless you sign in and use a feature that explicitly shares them, such as sharing a drive with friends.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data. We do not keep a history of where you have been. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not rank, publish or compare riders’ speeds.

Third parties

Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Google provides maps, Street View and sign-in (subject to Google’s privacy policy). Routing uses the OSRM project. Each receives only what is needed to provide its service.

Your choices

You can use Joyride without an account. You can delete your account and its data from the profile screen or by emailing us. You can turn off location access in your phone settings at any time; the app then works as a planning tool.
