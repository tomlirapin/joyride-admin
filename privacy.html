Privacy Policy

Updated November 9, 2026

Terms of Use

What we collect

If you sign in: your email address, and for Google sign-in the name and profile picture URL Google provides (we do not display the picture). Your chosen avatar, ride and driving style. Friends you accept into your crew see your driver name, avatar and, if you entered one, your first name; other riders never see your first name. Road ratings and surface notes you submit, tied to a random device identifier rather than your name. With your permission, your location while the app is open, used on the device to show the map around you.

Location and friends

Being friends never shows anyone where you are. Your location is shared only inside a Joyride 2GTHR ride you choose to join: a leader invites you, and you must tap Join within 15 minutes. While the ride is on, only the riders in it see your spot on the map. When the ride ends (the leader ends it, its time runs out, or the leader’s route ends), sharing stops and every position from the ride is erased. If you share presence, your account also notes a rough area (about 7 miles across) for counting riders by region; it is never shown to other riders and never as an exact spot. Friends see when you last opened the app, never where.

Photo avatars

If you choose to make your avatar from a photo, that one photo is sent to OpenAI, which draws the cartoon. We do not keep the photo; OpenAI may hold it for up to 30 days for safety checks and does not use it to train its models. Only the finished cartoon is saved, and other riders can see it. Pick a different avatar at any time to stop using it.

What stays on your phone

Recorded drives (route, times, speeds and cornering), your own ratings list, and preferences are stored on your phone. They are not uploaded unless you sign in and use a feature that explicitly shares them, such as sharing a drive with friends.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data. We do not keep a history of where you have been. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not rank, publish or compare riders’ speeds.

Drive reports (testing)

While Joyride is being tested, you can choose to share a drive report each time you drive with directions. We ask once before your first drive, and nothing is recorded unless you say yes. A report holds how smoothly the app ran (frames each second and any freezes), where on the screen you tapped and what was open, what the voice said (directions are kept only as “a direction”, never the street names), offers and legs, the app version and your phone model. It never holds your location, the places you drove or your destination. Nothing is sent while you drive: the report is sent once, when the drive ends. Reports are tied to your account so we can match them to a screenshot you send, are seen only by the Joyride team, are used only to find and fix problems, and are deleted after 90 days. Turn reports off at any time in ••• More while driving, and ask us to delete yours.

Reports and screenshots

When you send a report, we get your message, your email, the screen you were on and a rough location, so we can follow up. If you add screenshots, they are stored privately and only the Joyride team can open them. We use them only to fix the problem you reported. Ask us and we will delete a report and its screenshots.

Third parties

Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Mapbox draws the map, plans routes and finds places when you search (it receives what you type and the area you’re in). Google provides Street View, the map beside it in Preview, the natural voice for spoken directions (it receives only the words, never who you are) and Google sign-in (subject to Google’s privacy policy). OpenAI draws photo avatars when you ask for one. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that sign-ins and sign-ups come from a person, not a bot; it runs in the background and collects only what that check needs, as described in Cloudflare’s Turnstile Privacy Addendum (https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/). Each receives only what is needed to provide its service.

Your choices

You can use Joyride without an account. You can leave a Joyride 2GTHR ride at any time. You can delete your account and its data in Settings → Contact support, or by emailing us. You can turn off location access in your phone settings at any time; the app then works as a planning tool.
